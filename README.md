**AEROCUP — The Anti-Gravity Mug.** A catalogue photograph of an impossible
gadget: a cream ceramic mug that floats in mid-air while its coffee pours
*uphill* from the saucer below, over the rim and into the cup. 350 ml,
weighs 0 g, hovers at 12 cm, $249.

`main.py` renders the whole shot with nothing but the Python standard
library — a hand-written rasteriser (spans, gradients, soft shadows, a
small stroke font) writes a 1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`.
No network, no third-party packages, deterministic.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The shot

- Hero: the floating AEROCUP mug, studio-lit on a clean warm backdrop.
- The impossible job, visible at a glance: the coffee stream rises from
  the saucer, curves over the rim and dives into the cup — with a ripple
  where it lands and droplets suspended mid-air.
- Exactly one accessory: the saucer the coffee climbs out of.
- Catalogue furniture: brand wordmark, two spec callouts
  ("POURS UPHILL", "HOVERS AT 12 CM"), product name and spec line.
